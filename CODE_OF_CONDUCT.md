# Code of conduct

BurpBridge welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Treat participants with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces.

- Give constructive feedback about the work and accept corrections respectfully.
- Help newcomers understand the project and its contribution requirements.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's private information.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or use sexualized language or imagery.
- Do not sustain personal attacks, trolling, or disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban
participants when necessary. Enforcement should consider severity, impact, and
repeated behavior, and give participants a chance to clarify when appropriate.

Report concerns privately to an organization owner using the contact information
on their GitHub profile. Include links and relevant context, and avoid repeating
private information publicly. Maintainers must handle reports confidentially and
recuse themselves when personally involved.
