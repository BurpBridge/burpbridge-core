# Developer Certificate of Origin

BurpBridge requires certification under the unmodified [Developer Certificate of Origin, version 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/).

Read the certificate at that authoritative link before contributing. Add a matching `Signed-off-by: Your Name <your-email>` trailer to each commit with `git commit -s`. This records your certification that you have the right to contribute the work under the project's license and understand that the contribution and sign-off become a public record.

The project also requires a verified GPG signature, created with `git commit -S`. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for setup and repair instructions.
